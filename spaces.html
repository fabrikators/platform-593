Fabrika · Beautiful spaces — Achrafieh, Beirut

A floor of white marble, with two terraces.

Platform 593 is a first floor in Achrafieh, connected to Fabrika: a 135 m² hall drawn through by one black line, a glass meeting room, a big room, a kitchen and 115 m² of terraces, one of them under a steel pergola. A base for a team, a room for a gathering.

01 — The space

One open floor, rooms where you need them.

The hall runs the length of the building, from the balcony windows on the north façade to full-height glazing onto the pergola terrace. A glass meeting room, a closed big room, a kitchen and storage sit around it, so a team can work in the open and still close a door.

The floor is white marble with a single black inlay line that loops through the hall.

      05 — With Fabrika

      Part of Fabrika.

      Platform 593 is one of the spaces connected to Fabrika, a production platform for social, cultural and creative projects in Beirut. Since 2016 Fabrika has hosted more than 150 organisations.

      Fabrika’s own building, 2,000 m² over six levels, adds offices, an event space, studios and a library.
